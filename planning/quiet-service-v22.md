## Sound sources

- `assets/sfx/pour-v22.wav`: an individual CC0 sound **Pouring Liquid.wav** by **Dvideoguy**, Freesound sound207781, https://freesound.org/people/Dvideoguy/sounds/207781/ . The author's page identifies this individual sound as CC0. Obtained from its public ESC-50 mirror at https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50/blob/master/audio/5-207781-A-17.wav . Only this individually CC0 sound is used; the ESC-50 collection as a whole has a separate noncommercial dataset license and is not incorporated. Its license inventory explicitly identifies207781 as CC0. The original2.832s sound was padded to5s in the mirror; this game trims trailing silence to2.8s, applies85Hz high-pass/5600Hz low-pass, short fades and a conservative peak limiter. Mono44.1kHz PCM16.
- `assets/sfx/boil-v22.wav`: **water_boiling.ogg**, from **rubberduck**'s **30 CC0 SFX loops**, https://opengameart.org/content/30-cc0-sfx-loops . Downloaded from the independent public CC0 mirror https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds/blob/main/30-cc0-sfx-loops/water_boiling.ogg . Repository CC0 declaration retained at `assets/sfx/CC0.txt`. Mono44.1kHz PCM16,75Hz high-pass/4200Hz low-pass, short fades/limiter. The source's production method is not asserted.

These local files replace the former procedural noise stream for liquid/soy/dashi/broth and boiling. Egg mixture and oil use the liquid source with softer filtering. Pouring only sounds during the physical drag/hold; the commit does not replay a second pour. Sound stops on pointer release, completion, stove off or leaving the kitchen. Rice-washing one-shots have short bounded duration. Frying's steady noise bed is reduced. Missing/undecoded liquid assets remain silent rather than falling back to noise.

The finished-dish cue is an original1.25s softly decaying tuned bell sequence, played once per dish completion. Audio output, decoding, levels, routing and silence are checked with Chromium and sample data. This environment does not support subjective audio listening; no direct audition is claimed.
