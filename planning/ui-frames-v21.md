# v21 real walnut frame

`docs/assets/ui-frame-walnut-v21.webp` is a generated, straight-on, mitered walnut frame. Its opening has true alpha transparency; no paper, portrait or UI content is baked into it. Four grain-aligned rails have natural pores, fine wear, miter seams, inner recesses and illuminated bevels. The 1254 × 1254 PNG source is `/workspace/generated_images/exec-b3b0d605-04b0-444c-be72-0ac3d25b956c.png`, created with image_gen for this game. It was encoded without cropping, resizing or visual edits to WebP quality90/method6, preserving alpha (approximately207 KiB).

`docs/frames.css` uses CSS nine-slice border-image at10.2%. Major paper folios get an8–10px outer walnut frame; small wood HUD plaques get4–5px and the dock6–7px. The bottom conversation card has a top/side frame; its separate guest portrait remains untouched. Existing washi backgrounds and readable text remain independent. Inner cards, speech balloons, choices, item art, intro and scenes are not framed or filtered.

Load frames.css after analog.css. Tactile control styles may follow it because those styles own buttons while this file owns outer containers. Precache frames.css and ui-frame-walnut-v21.webp. Relative local URLs work offline.

Browser validation evidence: `/workspace/work/midnight-diner/v21-motion/frames-*` (320,393 and430px widths, major sheet and dialogue, finger targets, overflow). No cooking imagery was generated or changed.
